# Case studies and postmortems: small/solo Roblox launches on small budgets (2023–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-01. Network note: devforum.roblox.com, reddit.com, create.roblox.com, about.roblox.com, sec.gov, substack, gameanalytics.com, bloxg.com, guptamedia.com, rolearn.dev and profitable.app were all blocked for direct fetch; every DevForum/SEC/analyst figure below comes from WebSearch result summaries of those pages and is cited by the result URL. Treat exact numbers from forum threads as community-reported (self-reported by the poster) unless labeled official.

Label key used throughout: **[official]** = Roblox corporate/SEC/newsroom/DevForum staff announcement; **[community]** = self-reported by a developer on DevForum/Reddit/X; **[estimate]** = third-party tracker, analyst, agency or vendor figure; **[inference]** = my own calculation from cited numbers.

## Key question 1: Postmortems from small developers who spent ~$100–$2,000 on launch marketing — what happened?

### Takeaway
Across every small-budget postmortem found (2023–2026), paid Roblox sponsored traffic was cheap to buy (roughly 3–20 Robux ≈ $0.01–$0.07 per play) but almost never recouped directly, because D1 retention of ad-sourced players was typically single digits and revenue per visit was far below cost per play; the few cases that worked used ads only as a seed to trigger Home recommendations, and that only happened when first-session retention was already strong before spending.

### Cited Findings

**Failures / negative ROI (solo or very small teams)**
- "I worked on a project for 2 years. It failed" (DevForum, 2025): solo dev, 6–12 h/day for ~2 years on a "Plates of Fate but with building" game; playtests with friends/randoms were "highly positive"; advertised with a **35,000 Robux budget**; the best cost achieved was **~0.010 ad credit per visit (≈ $0.01/visit)** after iterating images/text; the diagnosed killer: players finished the tutorial and left, and **~70% would not wait one minute in a queue** to join a round. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3919166](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-worked-on-a-project-for-2-years-it-failed/3919166)
- "Low Retention and No Monetization led my group to lose 20k on ads" (DevForum, 2024): game "Kaboom Kompetition", liked by small streamers/friends; released a monetization update, then 2 weeks later ran a **20,000 Robux (70 ad credit)** campaign targeting PC + console, age 9+, deliberately excluding mobile/tablet; result: retention "much lower than expected" even though average session time matched baseline; the campaign was a net loss. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3076020](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-retention-and-no-monetization-led-my-group-to-lose-20k-on-ads/3076020)
- "Roblox ads are a money drain that can only be effectively used by cashgrabs" (DevForum, 2025): dev reinvested commission earnings into ads at **~3–4 Robux per user**; ad-sourced players joined for **~50 seconds** and left; **ARPDAU fell from 0.3 to 0.03 Robux**; the poster's rule: do not advertise a passion project, and only advertise once Analytics shows **≥50% of new users still in session at the 5-minute mark**. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3637858](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-ads-are-a-money-drain-that-can-only-be-effectively-used-by-cashgrabs/3637858)
- "Why has our game failed?" (DevForum, 2024): roleplay game, 2 years in development, advertised and still failed; a reply reported "**over 3K Robux** ... maybe **70K+ impressions and only 200+ players**"; diagnosis in-thread: with only ~500 visits the game never reached the recommendation algorithm and is essentially unfindable by search. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 2921427](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-has-our-game-failed/2921427)
- "Optimizing my game's Cost Per Play from Ads" (DevForum, 2024): group-based game "Seal's Kingdom"; a few thousand Robux spent; **cost per play $0.02 (~5.7 Robux)**; if half joined the group that is 11.4 Robux per member, judged unsustainable; audience mix of sponsored traffic was 57% US / 12.5% UK; 38% age 18+, 18.6% 13–17, 23.9% 9–12, 20.6% under 9; most play on tablet, then PC, then iPhone; a reply suggested bidding $0.01 for mobile audiences. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3047197](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/optimizing-my-games-cost-per-play-from-ads/3047197)
- "15k visits, 4.3% CTR, near-zero retention — what am I missing?" (DevForum, 2026): hidden-killer round game reached **15K visits, 4.3% ad CTR, peak 40 CCU** but almost nobody returned after one game; adding a tutorial, auto-queue and audio feedback did not move retention. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 4871576](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/15k-visits-43-ctr-near-zero-retention-%E2%80%94-what-am-i-missing/4871576)
- "Game failed and I need advice" (DevForum, July 2025): "Knockout Battles" had almost no returning players, sat at **2–3 players every few hours**, and made **no Robux despite heavy monetization**; feedback: needs ideas that are fresh but recognizably close to established games. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3829173](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-failed-and-i-need-advice-on-how-to-do-better/3829173)
- "I Released my new game but it was a flop" (DevForum, Nov 2025): long-development solo game flopped; replies blamed no tutorial (players get lost) and zero promotion; suggested Robux sponsorship or small/medium streamers. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 4089941](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-released-my-new-game-but-it-was-a-flop-why/4089941)
- Older but illustrative (pre-2023, flag): one developer spent **17K Robux over 2 days** on sponsors + ads for **600 visits**; another reported console sponsors at **0% CTR**; another reported **2M+ Robux lifetime** on sponsored/user ads with D1 retention averaging ~12% and "no significant avail". **[community]** — [DevForum: Lack of clicks + visits](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/lack-of-clicks-visits-on-advertisements-and-sponsors/1776350); [DevForum: Unusual Low Retention](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unusual-low-retention/2545813)
- Budget-mechanics failure modes: "2 Sponsors immediately spent my entire budget" (budget gone within ~1 hour) and "Sponsored ads stopping after just a few hours" (40K Robux across two ads, both stopped after hours, delivering only a fraction of paid plays). **[community]** — [DevForum thread 2941115](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2-sponsors-immediately-spent-my-entire-budget/2941115); [DevForum thread 1974917](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-stopping-after-just-a-few-hours-wasting-all-of-the-spent-on-them/1974917)

**Larger-spend reference points (not solo-scale, but show what the money buys)**
- "Foot Battles" launched with **~100K Robux** of sponsorships; Roblox Analytics percentiles reported by the dev: Avg session **8.2 min (67th pct)**, payer conversion **1.45% (84th pct)**, ARPPU **130.1 Robux (71st pct)**, but **D1 retention 5.22% (49th pct)**. **[community, percentiles from official Analytics benchmarks]** — [DevForum thread 3307270](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-game-statistics-d1-retention/3307270)
- Another dev put **100K Robux in sponsors + 50K in ads for one day → ~6K visits**, D1 "horrible". **[community]** — [DevForum: Low Retention on Experience (D1)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-retention-on-experience-d1/2387650)
- Launch-budget advice thread (2025): consensus floor "no less than **$100** for a single sponsored campaign"; one dev planned **~30,000 Robux (~$100) for a whole week** to generate enough data to test algorithm fit; another grew with **$0 ads** via free-weekend "try before you buy" promotions over 18 months; several noted promotion got harder/more expensive after Roblox removed classic User Ads. **[community]** — [DevForum thread 3667546](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-launch-advertising-cost/3667546)

**General postmortem lessons (small devs)**
- "Why Games Fail, and How To Fix It": "99% of projects will not succeed"; recurring causes: too-late playtesting, no load testing, unprepared moderation vs exploiters, thin shops, unrealistic expectation that a first game will make you rich. **[community tutorial]** — [DevForum thread 1588819](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-games-fail-and-how-to-fix-it-a-comprehensive-tutorial/1588819)
- "So your game died..." (2024): typical pattern is thousands of Robux into a dev team + ads and then no reflection; prescriptions: define what is unique vs genre peers, sell the concept not generic ads, the market is saturated so innovation is mandatory, "trends die quickly but communities last longer — a small loyal playerbase beats a brief flash". **[community tutorial]** — [DevForum thread 2644450](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-your-game-died-a-thread-on-game-design-and-the-market/2644450)
- A solo dev's Medium postmortem (non-Roblox-specific but applicable): marketing failed because it started too late (should begin ~6 months pre-launch) and community management collapsed for lack of manpower. **[community]** — [Medium postmortem](https://medium.com/kolektif-gamedev/post-mortem-publishing-my-first-ambitious-game-as-a-solo-developer-kind-of-3c468e9270d2)

### Inferences
- Observed cost per sponsored play across these threads clusters at **~3–20 Robux ($0.01–$0.07)**. The community-cited revenue benchmark is **~0.5 Robux per visit** (well-monetized games 3–8 R$/visit; see KQ2). Therefore direct recoup from sponsored traffic alone requires RPV ≥ CPP, i.e., ≥3–20 R$/visit from cold ad traffic — which none of the small-budget cases achieved. Paid ads on Roblox only "pay back" when they trigger organic Home-recommendation traffic. **[inference]**
- The common failure pattern is not "ads didn't deliver plays" (they usually did, cheaply) but "players left in under a minute" — i.e., the money reveals a retention problem rather than solving it. Every positive-feedback-from-friends case still failed on strangers, so friend playtests are not predictive. **[inference]**
- At the user's ad budget (₪1,500 ≈ $400 ≈ 400 ad credits if bought with cash, or ~114 credits if converted from ~32K Robux at 285 R$/credit), the realistic purchase is **roughly 6,000–40,000 sponsored plays** depending on bid ($0.01–$0.07 CPP). That is enough to generate a statistically meaningful D1 read, but not enough to sustain CCU for more than a few days. **[inference]**

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/robloxgamedev threads could be retrieved (domain blocked and WebSearch returned no relevant Reddit results for ad-spend postmortems); all small-budget cases are DevForum.
- No small-dev postmortem gave a complete chain of budget → visits → D1 → Robux earned → recouped yes/no in one place; numbers above are partial by thread.
- No found case of a solo developer who recouped a ~$500 sponsored campaign directly; absence in search is not proof it never happens.

## Key question 2: Base rates — what share of experiences reach visit milestones, and how is creator income distributed?

### Takeaway
Official data confirm an extreme power law: of millions of monetizing creators, only ~35,000 are in DevEx, the median DevEx creator received about $1,550 in 2025, and the top 1,000 averaged $1.3M; third-party estimates say the median experience earns nothing and the top 1% take over half of revenue. No source publishes the share of new experiences reaching 1K/10K/100K/1M visits.

### Cited Findings
- **[official]** Of the millions of creators monetizing on Roblox, **over 35,000** are in DevEx; the **median DevEx creator received $1,550** in the twelve months ended Dec 31, 2025. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- **[official]** In 2025 the **top 1,000 creators earned an average of $1.3M**, growing 50% YoY; **>100 developers earned >$1M** over the trailing 12 months; total creator earnings in 2025 **>$1.5B**. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm); [Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- **[official]** Creators earned >$1B from March 2024–March 2025 (+31% YoY). — [Netinfluencer summary of Roblox announcement](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-creators-earn-over-1b-as-platform-drives-economic-growth-worldwide/)
- **[official]** DevEx rate raised 8.5% from Sept 5, 2025; Creator Rewards program replaced EBP from July 24, 2025 (pays Robux for engagement/monetization metrics). — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-creators-will-now-earn-85-more-from-developer-exchange/)
- **[official, April 2026]** Roblox raised the qualifying DevEx rate by 42% for high-fidelity games aimed at 18+ players. — [Roblox newsroom Apr 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
- **[official]** Roblox states that the majority of the top 1,000 experiences were built by teams averaging fewer than 10 people. — [RDC 2025 newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025)
- **[estimate]** "More than 40 million experiences on Roblox; the vast majority earn zero Robux; the median game generates no revenue"; top 10 creators capture ~30% of top-1,000 earnings. — [RoWatcher News](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- **[estimate]** Top 1% of experiences captured **53.6% of monthly revenue**; concentration is increasing; top 10 games ≈ $40–90M combined monthly revenue. — [InvestGame 2025 Roblox Benchmark Report](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/the-2025-roblox-benchmark-report/); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- **[estimate]** ~7 million daily active experiences (Q3 2025); top 10 experiences passed 180B cumulative visits in 2025 (+22% YoY). — [Rolearn 2025 Annual Review](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/2025-annual-review/); [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/roblox-stats)
- **[estimate, likely stale]** "5,000 games on Roblox have over a million visits" appears in Wikipedia's list page; date of that count is unclear and probably predates 2023. — [Wikipedia: List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- **[estimate]** Retention benchmarks: GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox Benchmark (>500 games, >1M MAU each, Aug 2025–Jul 2026): **median D1 = 10.3%**, median session **7.28 min**, 75th-percentile session **14.85 min**. — [GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report); [RRQ World summary](https://blog.rrqworld.com/roblox-game-retention/)
- **[estimate]** Newzoo (2025): "only 12–15% of users return after the first day" on Roblox. — [GameDevReports: Newzoo Roblox 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)
- **[estimate]** Roblox Analytics official percentile shown to a dev: D1 **5.22% ≈ 49th percentile** (i.e., the median game in that benchmark set had ~5% D1). — [DevForum thread 3307270](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-game-statistics-d1-retention/3307270)
- **[estimate]** Revenue per visit: community benchmark ≈ **0.5 Robux/visit** (≈ $0.00122/visit after 30% marketplace cut at $0.0035 DevEx ≈ **$1,225 per 1M visits**); "well-monetized" games 3–8 R$/visit. — [Meshlox calculator](https://meshlox.com/tool/roblox-calculator); [Profitable.app calculator](https://profitable.app/tools/roblox-revenue-calculator)
- **[estimate]** In-experience (immersive) ad revenue is negligible for devs: fractions of a cent to $0.55 per thousand visits; Roblox keeps 70%. — [MEXC news summary](https://www.mexc.com/news/177474)

### Inferences
- The $1,550 median is for creators who already cleared DevEx's cash-out threshold, so it is an upper bound on the "typical" monetizing creator; the typical new experience earns 0. **[inference]**
- Recouping the user's full $530 via DevEx at $0.0035/Robux requires ≈ **151,000 earned Robux**; at 0.5 R$/visit that is ≈ **300,000 visits**; at 3 R$/visit ≈ **50,000 visits**. Both exceed what $400 of sponsored traffic buys (≈6K–40K plays), so recoup depends entirely on organic multiplication. **[inference]**
- Given median D1 ≈ 5–10%, a new solo game with D1 ≥ 15–20% would sit near the top quartile and is the minimum profile at which ad spend has historically triggered recommendation traffic. **[inference]**

### Gaps
- No official or analyst source gives the distribution of visits for new experiences (share reaching 1K/10K/100K/1M). The only datapoint ("5,000 games >1M visits") is undated.
- DevEx's minimum cash-out threshold (30,000 earned Robux, per Roblox's DevEx docs) could not be fetched here (create.roblox.com blocked); report writer should treat it as known-official-but-unverified in this run.
- Roblox has not published how many of its ~35K DevEx creators are solo vs studio.

## Key question 3: Anime / battlegrounds / isekai genre — competitiveness, breakouts, lifetimes, what the audience responds to

### Takeaway
Fighting/anime is the fastest-growing and one of the most crowded genres in 2026, with the leaders (Jujutsu Shenanigans ~263K CCU, The Strongest Battlegrounds ~110K CCU) built by teenagers or tiny teams years ago; the genre's audience is driven by codes, race/spin rerolls and update hype, lifetimes are long for leaders but even 300K-CCU titles (Blue Lock Rivals) collapsed within a year after ownership change and a mishandled update, and IP risk (DMCA) is real.

### Cited Findings
- **[estimate]** Fighting games are the fastest-growing genre on Roblox in 2026, driven by the anime trend. — [KitsBlox genre report](https://kitsblox.com/blog/popular-roblox-game-genres-2026)
- **[estimate]** 2026 leaders: Jujutsu Shenanigans **263K concurrent**; The Strongest Battlegrounds **110K concurrent**. — [Pocket Tactics anime games 2026](https://www.pockettactics.com/roblox-anime-games)
- **[community/wiki]** Jujutsu Shenanigans was built by "Tze", who started scripting/VFX at 14 and launched the game at 15; a solo/teen-led project. — [Roblox Fandom: Tze's Shenanigans](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tze's_Shenanigans); [Shapes bio](https://shapes.inc/maybetze)
- **[estimate]** The Strongest Battlegrounds (Yielding Arts, created Aug 2022): **~17–19B visits**, est. **$438K/month** revenue, Yielding Arts est. **$154M lifetime**; won 2 Roblox Innovation Awards 2024 (Best Fighting, Best Strategy). — [RoWatcher TSB stats](https://rowatcher.com/games/3808081382/tech-prodigy-the-strongest-battlegrounds); [Roblox Fandom: Yielding Arts](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Yielding_Arts); [Bloxy News on X](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/1812532039310348333?lang=en)
- **[wiki]** Heroes Battlegrounds (MHA-themed; creator "adjutet"/"more awesome games yo"), created April 11, 2023, passed **1B visits by June 2025**. — [Roblox Fandom: Heroes Battlegrounds](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/More_awesome_games_yo/Heroes_Battlegrounds)
- **[tracker]** Blue Lock: Rivals created July 26, 2024, **4.9B+ visits**. — [Rolimons: Blue Lock Rivals](https://www.rolimons.com/game/18668065416)
- **[community press]** Blue Lock Rivals decline: peak **>300K CCU**; ownership changed April 2025 (Chrollo's team left, Miro's team took over); the "Loki update" + Masters rarity is called "the beginning of the end"; CCU fell under 100K after it, with long-standing bugs unfixed while new features shipped; now ~**14–15K CCU**; competing causes: the original dev's new game Soccer Zero, and platform attention shifting to Grow a Garden / 99 Nights in the Forest. — [ProGameGuides: Is Blue Lock Rivals dying?](https://progameguides.com/roblox/is-blue-lock-rivals-dying-rise-of-soccer-zero-what-it-means-for-fans/); [Earnaldo comparison](https://earnaldo.com/blog/blue-lock-rivals-vs-volleyball-legends)
- **[press]** IP risk: Anime Adventures (Gomu), Anime Fighting Simulator and AFS X (BlockZone) were taken down/privated after DMCA requests attributed to Gamefam, which holds the official My Hero Academia license with Crunchyroll (2023–2024). — [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-adventures/private)
- **[press]** Isekai-style titles active in 2025–2026 all run race-roll/reroll economies: The Forge (race spin; World 2 quests give rerolls), The Veil (launch codes RELEASE / VERITYBOMB give race rerolls as of Sept 2026), Tensura Incremental (race/skill/spirit rolls), Gakuran; common race ~25% chance vs rare races 0.5%. — [games.gg The Forge race reroll](https://games.gg/news/the-forge-race-reroll-on-roblox/); [GamesRadar The Veil codes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/rpg/the-veil-codes/); [PCGamesN Tensura Incremental codes](https://www.pcgamesn.com/tensura-incremental/codes); [PCGamesN Gakuran codes](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/gakuran-codes)
- **[press]** Codes tied to like-count milestones (e.g., Anime Spirits "30KLIKES", "35KLIKESYESS") rewarding race/perk spins are standard; codes articles on PCGamesN, Pocket Tactics, GameRant, ProGameGuides are a recurring free discovery channel for anime titles. — [TryHardGuides Anime Spirits codes](https://tryhardguides.com/anime-spirits-codes-race-perks-spins-for-free/); [Pocket Tactics Anime Dice codes](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-dice-codes); [GameRant Spin a Anime codes](https://gamerant.com/roblox-spin-a-anime-codes/)
- **[community]** Roblox's own 2026 discovery guidance explicitly says thumbnails that "win attention" without long-term value were being demoted — relevant because anime battlegrounds compete heavily on flashy thumbnails. — [Roblox newsroom "Optimizing Discovery"](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)

### Inferences
- The genre's winners were first-movers (TSB 2022, Heroes 2023, JJS ~2023–24) on a specific IP/mechanic, not late entrants with better visuals; a 2026 isekai entrant competes against entrenched 100K+ CCU titles and dozens of race-spin RPGs. **[inference]**
- The Blue Lock Rivals case shows the genre audience punishes update mishandling fast (300K → <100K CCU in months) and rewards continuous balance/content cadence; for a solo dev, the lesson is that post-launch cadence matters more than launch polish. **[inference]**
- Cinematic cutscenes are not something any found source credits for anime-game growth; what the audience demonstrably responds to is race/spin rerolls, like-milestone codes, and content-creator coverage of new characters/moves. **[inference]**
- Use of named anime IP (JJK, MHA, Blue Lock) carries takedown risk; original "isekai-style" content avoids that but loses the search traffic that licensed-IP names attract. **[inference]**

### Gaps
- No source quantified how many anime/battlegrounds games launch per month or what share reach 1K CCU; "saturated" is asserted by community posts, not measured.
- No postmortem was found for a small anime game that "died quickly despite good visuals" with numbers; Blue Lock Rivals is the only numerically documented anime decline, and it is a studio-scale case.
- Jujutsu Shenanigans revenue and launch-marketing details were not retrievable (tracker page blocked).

## Key question 4: Roblox Ads Manager ROI in 2025–2026 — cost per play, retention of sponsored traffic, and comparison with creators / TikTok

### Takeaway
Roblox's own bid guidance puts winning sponsored-experience bids at about $0.04–$0.07 per play (minimum 10 ad credits, 1 credit = 285 Robux), but developers consistently report that sponsored players retain and monetize far worse than organic ones — one 2026 bug report measured playtime of 11–12 min with ads on vs 28–30 min with ads off — and Roblox has since added per-source retention analytics; creator and TikTok costs per player are lower on paper but evidence for small games is thin and vendor-sourced.

### Cited Findings
- **[official]** Sponsored Experiences in Ads Manager bill per "play" (player joins after landing on the experience page); Roblox-stated winning bid range **$0.04–$0.07 per play**; bids of $0.01 "likely result in underdelivery or no delivery"; **1 ad credit = 285 Robux; minimum purchase 10 credits; minimum 5 credits/day for 2 days**. — [DevForum announcement: Sponsored Experiences moving to Ads Manager](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756); [DevForum: Enhancements to Sponsored Experiences](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/enhancements-to-sponsored-experiences-and-ads-manager/2972589)
- **[official, 2025]** "Leveling Up Ads Manager": bid insights added because campaigns were underdelivering on too-low bids; sponsored tiles moved higher on Home (tests showed significantly more impressions/clicks/plays per user); fractional bidding auto-optimizes for lowest cost per play; targeting by experience genre, audience genre and country. — [DevForum: Leveling Up Ads Manager](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/leveling-up-ads-manager-with-new-features/3587640)
- **[community]** One dev reported average cost per play as low as **$0.004** with max-bid settings (page 10 of the migration thread). — [DevForum migration thread p.10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756?page=10)
- **[community, Aug 29 2026 bug report]** "Sponsored Ads traffic degrades the engagement metrics used for discovery": with ads running, average playtime **11.1–12.0 min**; with ads off **28.0–29.9 min**; similar gaps in session length and D1; the poster argues paid acquisition therefore harms organic ranking because the ranking system does not separate paid from organic traffic. — [DevForum thread 4838351](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)
- **[official, Oct 16 2025]** Roblox Analytics now shows D1/D7/D30 retention broken down by acquisition source (Home recommendations vs other) and lets creators pick their benchmark set; Roblox notes retention differs by source. — [DevForum: Analytics retention by acquisition source](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157)
- **[community, 2026]** "Does sponsoring improve retention?": consensus that sponsoring is for getting initial visits into Home recommendations on a new game; "ads reach everyone which generally means the analytics for ads are bad"; not recommended unless the game gets no Home traffic. — [DevForum thread 4073864](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/does-sponsoring-improve-retention/4073864)
- **[community]** Ad-sourced players left after ~50 s; ARPDAU 0.3 → 0.03 Robux (see KQ1). — [DevForum thread 3637858](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-ads-are-a-money-drain-that-can-only-be-effectively-used-by-cashgrabs/3637858)

## Key question 5: What separated winners from losers — launch readiness, retention, update cadence, community, luck/timing; do/don't lists from experienced developers and studios

### Takeaway
The consistent separator is retention measured on strangers before spending, then update cadence that feeds the recommendation system; Roblox's 2025–2026 algorithm changes lengthened the evaluation window to 28 days and down-weighted clickbait, so launches are judged over weeks, not days — which both punishes thin games and gives slow starters a second chance.

### Cited Findings
**Platform/algorithm facts**
- **[official, June 15 2026]** Roblox's "Optimizing Discovery" rollout expanded Recommended-For-You evaluation from 7 to **28 days**, scoring retention in three buckets (D1, D2–7, D8–28), with CGO John Ciancutti stating that games winning attention with flashy thumbnails but lacking long-term value "were displacing games that players value for the long term". — [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [DevForum: RFY improvements valuing long-term retention](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575); [Yahoo Finance coverage](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/rblxs-discovery-shift-targets-retention-142600721.html)
- **[official]** Roblox's "Boost Your Discovery by Building Games People Want to Play" guidance: if retention is strong, build sustainable monetization; if retention is low, fix core gameplay first; headline signal is "intentional co-play days" (days a player deliberately plays with friends). — [DevForum thread 4779042](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042); [ZehnStudio26 summary](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-discovery-rollout/)
- **[official, 2026]** New discovery surfaces: "Moments" (gameplay clips from friends/creators with one-tap join), "Standout Games" sort for novel games chosen by the curation team via a nomination form (replacing Today's Picks). — [Roblox newsroom: Moments](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/07/moments-new-homepage-unlocks-gaming-for-all); [RDC 2026 newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)
- **[community]** The algorithm takes **2–4 weeks** to evaluate; a launch that looks dead in week one may simply lack 7-day data; once trailing signals mature it re-tests with a bigger audience — one game went from **~7,000 DAU to >126,000 DAU in eight days**. — [Games.gg algorithm coverage](https://games.gg/news/roblox-algorithm-adult-games-retention/)
- **[community, July 2026]** Portfolio operator EvanZir: his portfolio fell from ~489K DAU (June 14) to 290K (June 16, −40% in 48 h) after the RFY change, then recovered to ~552K DAU three weeks later with revenue ~17% above baseline; **147K of the 552K DAU came from new titles** launched around the change. — [EvanZir on X](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2074281929575174603)
- **[community, Apr 2026]** During an RFY algorithm test, some devs reported Home impressions and CCU dropping 70–80% — i.e., platform-level volatility is a real luck factor. — [DevForum thread 4576807](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/home-recommendation-impressions-and-ccu-dropped-70-80-following-rfy-algorithm-test-april-10-2026/4576807)

## Key question 6 (synthesis for the user's three scenarios): How realistic are "collapse but roughly recoup", "earn back more than invested", and "runs for months"?

### Takeaway
On the evidence, the user's worst case is too optimistic: a game that collapses within days essentially never recoups a ~$530 spend, because sponsored traffic alone yields far less revenue than it costs; "earn back more than invested" is a top-quartile outcome that requires retention well above the platform median plus organic recommendation lift; "runs for months" is what a minority of titles with sustained updates achieve, and in the anime genre it has been done by solo/teen developers, but as first-movers.

### Cited Findings
- Revenue vs cost per visit: community RPV benchmark ~0.5 Robux/visit (3–8 for well-monetized) vs sponsored cost 3–20 Robux/play. — [Meshlox](https://meshlox.com/tool/roblox-calculator); [DevForum 3637858](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-ads-are-a-money-drain-that-can-only-be-effectively-used-by-cashgrabs/3637858); [DevForum 3047197](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/optimizing-my-games-cost-per-play-from-ads/3047197)
- Ad-sourced players' ARPDAU collapsed to 0.03 Robux in one case; a heavily monetized failed game made zero Robux at 2–3 CCU. — [DevForum 3637858](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-ads-are-a-money-drain-that-can-only-be-effectively-used-by-cashgrabs/3637858); [DevForum 3829173](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-failed-and-i-need-advice-on-how-to-do-better/3829173)
- Median DevEx creator $1,550/yr; median experience earns nothing. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- Algorithm can multiply a retentive game 18x in 8 days; evaluation window 2–4 weeks / 28 days. — [Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-algorithm-adult-games-retention/); [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- Solo/teen-built anime titles reaching 100K–260K CCU exist (JJS, TSB, Heroes Battlegrounds) but launched 2022–2024. — [Roblox Fandom: Tze](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tze's_Shenanigans); [RoWatcher TSB](https://rowatcher.com/games/3808081382/tech-prodigy-the-strongest-battlegrounds); [Roblox Fandom: Heroes Battlegrounds](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/More_awesome_games_yo/Heroes_Battlegrounds)

### Inferences
- **Worst case (collapse in days–weeks) — realistic probability: high (consistent with "99% fail" community base rate and the median-earns-zero estimate); recoup in that case — realistic probability: very low.** With $400 buying ~6K–40K plays at median D1 (5–10%) and RPV of ~0.5 R$/visit, expected gross is on the order of 3,000–20,000 Robux (~$10–70 at DevEx), i.e., 2–13% of the spend, and below DevEx's cash-out minimum. The user should plan on the worst case being a total loss of the ad slice. **[inference]**
- **Likely case ("earn back more than invested") — realistic probability: low-to-moderate, conditional.** It requires ≈150K+ earned Robux, i.e., ~50K–300K visits, which only organic Home/Trending traffic can supply; the gating condition visible in every case is first-session retention far above median before spending. If pre-launch tests with strangers show D1 < 10% or 5-minute retention < 50%, this scenario is effectively unavailable; if D1 ≥ 20%, it becomes plausible within the 28-day window. **[inference]**
- **Best case (runs for months) — realistic but rare**; the documented route is a retentive core loop + weekly content/race/code updates + creator coverage, not marketing spend; expect the genre to demand sustained post-launch work (Blue Lock Rivals lost two-thirds of its CCU within months once cadence/quality slipped). **[inference]**
- Budget allocation implication: the ₪1,500 ad slice should be released gradually (e.g., 5–10 credits/day over 2–4 weeks) as a diagnostic and seed, kept off until stranger-retention tests pass; the ₪300 creator slice has the weakest evidence of return at micro scale; the ₪200 social slice overlaps with free channels (codes articles, TikTok clips, Moments) that documented anime titles actually use. **[inference]**

- No found dataset gives the probability that a new experience with a given D1 reaches a given visit count, so the probabilities above are qualitative rankings derived from base rates and case patterns, not measured frequencies.
- No 2025–2026 solo anime/isekai launch postmortem with full financials was found; the closest analogues are non-anime solo postmortems and studio-scale anime declines.
