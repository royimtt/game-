# Roblox Discovery (2026) and the Economics of Paid Acquisition for a ~$530 Indie Budget

Research date: 1 October 2026. Labels used throughout: **[Official]** = Roblox docs, DevForum staff announcements, SEC filings / shareholder letters; **[Community]** = developer-reported on DevForum/X or in creator-tool blogs; **[Estimate]** = my arithmetic or a third party's modelled number. Official Roblox docs were read from the creator-docs GitHub source (`raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/...`); the same page is published at `create.roblox.com/docs/<path>`. DevForum, help-center and most blog pages were blocked by this machine's proxy, so DevForum facts come from search-engine summaries of those threads (URLs given) and should be re-checked by opening the thread.

Context for the writer: the user plans ~$400 on Roblox Ads Manager + ~$130 on social, expects "the algorithm" to amplify, and wants to at least recoup $530. Short verdict supported by the evidence below: (1) the algorithm does not reward paid traffic directly — it only scores *organic* Home-recommendation users, so ads can only help indirectly, by producing enough players for the "explore" phase to get real data; (2) $400 buys on the order of 900–1,800 sponsored plays at community-reported costs, which at typical D1 retention of 3–10% yields a few dozen returning players; (3) recouping $530 requires roughly 199,000 Robux of gross player spending (~$2,490 of Robux purchases) because of the 30% fee and the $0.0038 DevEx rate, and a 30,000-Robux DevEx minimum applies; (4) a brand-new experience is initially shown only to age-checked 16+ users until it gets 250 "highly engaged" age-checked plays in 60 days, which is a new 2026 gate that affects both ad targeting and organic reach.

## Key Question 1: What ranks experiences in Home "Recommended For You" in 2026, how Search/Charts/Today's Picks/new-experience exposure work, and whether a paid burst helps or hurts

### Takeaway
As of mid-2026 Home recommendations rank on organic post-click behaviour measured over 28 days (play-through rate and first-play bounce as the entry signals; play days and playtime per user in D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 windows as the heaviest signals; co-play, qualified sessions and spend as secondary), and **Roblox explicitly excludes users first acquired from ads, search, friends, curation or social media from the ranking stage** — so a paid burst cannot "feed the algorithm" directly, and a developer bug report (Aug 2026) argues it can actively drag down the engagement metrics the system reads. The only official mechanism for new experiences is an "explore and expand" phase seeded by "even a small number of people playing".

### Cited Findings

**The current ranking signals (official)**
- Roblox's Discovery doc describes a two-stage system: "Retrieval" selects "a subset of games that each user might enjoy playing based on key signals like engagement, retention, and monetization," then "Ranking" "selects the most relevant games to be ranked in a personalized way and shown to each user." — [Official: Discovery doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md) (published at https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- Signals listed as **most important**: "Play through rate — The rate at which users play your game after seeing it in the Recommended for You sort"; "First play bounce rate — The rate at which users leave your game after a short play session. This is a negative signal"; "Play days per user — The average number of unique days users engage with your game"; "Playtime per user — The average amount of time users spend in your game" (capped at 60 minutes per user per game per day). Signals listed as **important**: "Intentional co-play days per user — ... unique days that users come back to play your game with friends, including co-play days through join, invites, or private servers"; "Qualified play sessions per user — ... qualified play sessions per user who clicked and played your game through recommendations on the Home page. A 'qualified play' refers to a user's meaningful play session"; "Spend days per user"; "Robux spent per user". The doc adds: "Consider different time periods of D8-D28, D2-D7 and D1 for play days per user and playtime per user." — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- **Who is measured**: "Each signal is based on users who organically joined from the Recommended for You sort on Home." and "Roblox doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads, curation, friends, search, social media, or any other source in the ranking stage of Recommended for You." — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- The 60-minute daily playtime cap and the earlier 7-day signal set: the 2025 "Boost Your Discovery" announcement listed six signals — qualified play-through rate (qPTR), 7-day playtime per user ("maximum of 60 minutes per user, per experience, per day"), 7-day play days per user, 7-day spend days per user, 7-day Robux spent per user, 7-day intentional co-play days per user — and published their relative importance in Creator Analytics. — [Official: DevForum "Boost Your Discovery with the Improved Recommended For You Algorithm and Analytics for Creators"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) (2025; a search summary attributes a "global release" date of 11 Dec 2025 to this thread — unverified, see Gaps)
- **The 28-day update (what replaced qPTR)**: the announcement "Recommended For You Algorithm Improvements That Better Value Long-Term Retention" states the algorithm "is evolving to directly measure longer-term retention across Day 1, Day 2–7, and Day 8–28, using signals like playtime, play days, qualified play sessions, intentional co-play days, spend days, and Robux spent," that testing "not only increases user engagement through the first 28 days, but also into the future," and that it is "removing qualified play-through rate and adding play-through rate and first play bounce rates to better understand what happens immediately after a player decides to click on a game." Signals are surfaced in Creator Analytics. — [Official: DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575). Dating: the search engine placed the post "approximately 107 days ago" from a late-Sept/early-Oct 2026 crawl (≈ mid-June 2026), which matches the thread ID sequence and the Q2-2026 shareholder letter below; one third-party blog says "December 2025" — [Community: ZehnStudio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/) — treat the December date as unconfirmed.
- Community description of the metric change: "(old metric) QPTR = click on a specific thumbnail and play the game for 5 mins+; (new metric) PTR = click on a specific thumbnail and play the game; Q (Qualified) = First Play Bounce…" — [Community: X post by @ooStarwarsbccoo, June 2026](https://x.com/ooStarwarsbccoo/status/2066928795244712419)
- First-play bounce is reported in Creator Analytics in two brackets: "less than 60 seconds" and "61–180 seconds". — [Community: DevForum "First play bounce rate question"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/first-play-bounce-rate-question/4743249) (2026)
- Roblox's own investor commentary confirms the retention-first shift and its cost: the Q2 2026 shareholder letter attributes part of a bookings shortfall to "changes in our Recommended for You (RFY) algorithm, which intentionally provides more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near term monetization, and in Q2 the near-term impact on younger cohorts was larger than anticipated." Q2 2026: 123M DAUs (+10% YoY), 29B hours (+5%). — [Official: Roblox Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter (SEC 8-K exhibit)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); also [PDF](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- **RDC 2026 (10–12 Sept 2026)**: discovery "is being reworked to serve different audiences. The ranking system is becoming more age-aware, recommending shorter-form games to younger players and deeper, return-visit games to older players. It also looks past the first week, measuring engagement across the first 28 days and beyond, and Roblox is testing a 'direct growth' signal that tracks how many brand-new players a game brings to the platform." — [Official: Roblox IR press release, RDC 2026](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2026/Roblox-Unveils-New-Ways-to-Play-Build-and-Grow-at-the-Roblox-Developers-Conference-RDC/default.aspx); summarised by [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-rdc-every-major-announcement-for-players-and-creators/) and [Pocket Gamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/); event dates per [ZehnStudio26 RDC preview](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/rdc-2026-preview/)
- Volatility of the current algorithm for an established portfolio: a studio owner with 60+ games reported an algorithm change "cut his portfolio's revenue roughly in half overnight, with more than ten of his games dropping as much as 70% in an hour"; three weeks later the portfolio was at ~552,000 DAU with revenue ~17% above the pre-change baseline, driven by *new* titles, while "the old games that were hit by the change never came back." — [Community: @EvanZir, "I Was Wrong About the Roblox Algorithm (Mostly)", July 2026](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2074281929575174603)

**How a new experience gets its first organic exposure (official)**
- "Roblox's recommendation system uses explore and expand phases to understand the key signals." and "Games that have even a small number of people playing can signal to the system that the game is worth considering for distribution to more users who might find the game enjoyable through Recommended for You." — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- Four things move recommendation impressions: "Game updates and gameplay changes", "Algorithm modifications", "Seasonal variations (weekly, holiday, back-to-school patterns)", and "Competition from other successful games." — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- Metadata rules that *remove* an experience from prioritisation: "Leading with giveaways — Metadata that implies any type of monetary reward is not prioritized for recommendations"; "Mismatched metadata and content — ... is not recommended to users"; "Non-unique games — Games with metadata and place files that closely resemble existing games on Roblox are no longer prioritized for recommendations." Positive guidance: "Be accurate — Avoid using irrelevant keywords", "Use unique metadata — Focus on using original imagery and naming", "Avoid publishing content with repetitive titles and images that have been previously published." — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- **2026 audience gate (Roblox Kids / Roblox Select)**: new experiences are "first made available only to age-checked users 16 and older" until they reach "250 unique plays by highly engaged age-checked users within a 60-day window"; a highly engaged player is one who has "made a minimum purchase anywhere on Roblox in the last 60 days and have spent time in your game within that same window"; Roblox Kids = ages 5–8, Roblox Select = ages 9–15; progress is shown in the "Audience Reach dashboard in the Creator Hub." — [Official: Kids and Select doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/kids-and-select.md) (published at https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select). The threshold was cut from 500 to 250 in 2026 — [Official: DevForum "Highly Engaged Player Threshold Drops to 250"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164). Conflict: a search summary of community coverage says "when a game has had fewer than 100 highly engaged players over the last 60 days, users under 16 will no longer be able to play it" ([Community: RTC on X](https://x.com/Roblox_RTC/status/2090136001976951030); [BloxBot guide](https://bloxbot.ai/guide/roblox-new-publishing-requirements-2026)), whereas the official doc as fetched says that once eligible there is no ongoing threshold. Treat the maintenance rule as unresolved.

**Does a paid burst help or hurt ranking?**
- Official position (ads do not count toward ranking): see the "Roblox doesn't count ... users first acquired from ads" quote above. — [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- Official position (ads help launches): at RDC 2025 Roblox said "the 5 newest experiences to reach the top 20 by earnings all started with Sponsored Experience ads" and that "conversion lift studies showed that the typical experience running ads sees a 150% lift in impressions, a 24% increase in plays, and a 16% increase in playtime." — [Official: DevForum "Ads for Creators: RDC Recap and What's Next"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106) (Sept 2025; note "lift" is measured against the same experience without ads, not against organic discovery quality)
- Developer evidence that ads hurt the measured signals: a bug report filed 29 Aug 2026 for the extraction shooter "GET OUT" says running Sponsored Ads causes "measured playtime, session length, and D1 retention to fall by large margins" and "turning ads off causes all three to recover immediately"; with ads: average playtime 11.1–12 min, D1 ≈ 3%; without ads: 28.0–29.9 min, D1 5–8%; the author's thesis: "the advertising product is feeding traffic into the ranking product that does not behave like organic traffic, and nothing separates the two." — [Community: DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351). No Roblox staff resolution was visible in search results (Gap).
- Analyst framing of the same dilution effect for immersive-ad traffic: "a game with 20 percent acquisition from Immersive Ads and an average session length of 18 minutes would see their average session length drop by 18%." — [Community/analyst: Naavik, "Solving Roblox's Immersive Ads Problem"](https://naavik.co/digest/solvng-roblox-ads-problem/)
- Other developer threads reporting negative or null outcomes from paid campaigns: "Low Retention and No Monetization led my group to lose 20k on ads" — [Community: DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-retention-and-no-monetization-led-my-group-to-lose-20k-on-ads/3076020); "Why did buying an ad campaign decrease my playercount to 0?" — [Community: DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-did-buying-an-ad-campaign-decrease-my-playercount-to-0/3376248); "[Bug] Sponsored Ads Algorithm Fails to Target Users, Resulting in Mathematically Broken CTR (0.01%)" — [Community: DevForum, 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/bug-sponsored-ads-algorithm-fails-to-target-users-resulting-in-mathematically-broken-ctr-001/4734574); "Issues with Ad Campaign Traffic and Suspicious Player Behavior" — [Community: DevForum, 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/issues-with-ad-campaign-traffic-and-suspicious-player-behavior/4807120)
- Ads Manager doc warning: "Ad campaigns for games that are not unique can result in no ad spend and no conversions." — [Official: Ads Manager doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager.md)

### Inferences
- The user's assumption that "the Roblox algorithm will amplify the game" after a paid burst is **not supported**: ranking is computed only from users who arrived organically via Home, so paid plays neither raise play-through rate nor contribute play days/playtime to the score. The only indirect benefit is that paid players can (a) create concurrent servers so organic arrivals are not alone, (b) generate friends/co-play invitations that *do* count once a friend arrives organically, and (c) move the experience toward the 250 highly-engaged-player gate so under-16 users can play it at all.
- Conversely, the Aug 2026 bug report plus Naavik's arithmetic suggest the practical risk runs the other way: if Creator Analytics aggregates paid and organic sessions for the metrics Roblox *also* uses as inputs, a flood of low-intent paid traffic may lower the experience's apparent quality. Whether the ranking pipeline is truly isolated from this (as the doc states) or not (as the bug report claims) is unresolved; a cautious plan treats ads as a separate, measurable experiment rather than an algorithm primer.
- Because the 28-day windows now carry the most weight, a launch that produces strong D1 but weak D8–28 behaviour will lose impressions within weeks; the officially stated cure is progression/content depth, not more spend.
- For an anime/isekai combat game whose natural audience skews 13+, the 16+-only initial window is less damaging than for a kids' game, but the 250-highly-engaged-players gate still constrains the under-16 audience until met; sponsored "Engagement" campaigns exist specifically to buy progress toward it (see Key Question 3).

### Gaps
- Roblox has not published numeric weights for the 2026 signal set; "most important / important" tiers are the only official ordering. The 2025 relative-importance chart lived in the Creator Analytics UI and the DevForum thread (blocked here).
- The exact announcement date of the 28-day/qPTR-removal update could not be verified from the primary thread (DevForum blocked); evidence points to ~June 2026, one blog says December 2025.
- No official statement of how many organic impressions a brand-new experience receives in the "explore" phase, or how long it lasts, was found.
- Whether Roblox staff responded to the Aug 2026 "sponsored traffic degrades metrics" bug report is unknown.
- Search ranking factors beyond "semantic understanding" and the anti-clickbait rule are not documented.

## Key Question 2: Experience-page conversion (icon, thumbnails, video, title, description, genre, likes/favorites), A/B testing, and the Acquisition analytics view

### Takeaway
Roblox offers automatic thumbnail personalization (a bandit test across 2–5 thumbnails that lifted qualified play-through by +8.5% on average in Roblox's tests) but no icon A/B test; the Acquisition dashboard breaks new users down by source (Home recommendations, Today's Picks, search, sponsored, share links, etc.) with impressions → plays → D7 retention → 30-day payer conversion, and Roblox's stated success bar is "comparable or above your similar game benchmarks" for session time and D1 retention. Community benchmarks put a good sponsored-tile CTR at 1–2% (3%+ great) and median Roblox session length at ~9.8 minutes.

### Cited Findings

**Thumbnails, video and icons (official)**
- Up to 10 images or videos on the detail page; three sources: uploaded images (free), uploaded videos ("free with 3/month quota"), auto-generated; images "16:9 aspect ratio," ideally "1920×1080 pixels," formats .jpg/.gif/.png/.tga/.bmp, max 3 MB for Home-page personalization thumbnails. — [Official: Thumbnails doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/thumbnails.md) (published at https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- Video thumbnails must be "authentic gameplay video" that "accurately portray in-game content without misleading alterations"; allowed: free-camera cinematic angles, minor brightness/contrast edits, highlight-reel playthroughs, in-game UI text, Roblox catalog music; prohibited: misrepresented mechanics, "artificially enhancing graphics beyond what a player will experience," real-life footage, "voice-overs, narration, and music with lyrics," overlay text used as advertising or subjective claims. Not shown on Xbox, PlayStation or VR. — [Official: Thumbnails doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/thumbnails.md)
- Thumbnail personalization on Home: requires "2 or more thumbnails," recommended "2-5 thumbnails" active; the Thumbnails page reports impressions from Home recommendations, qualified plays, average playtime per qualified play, "Qualified Play Through Rate," and the winning audience segment; Roblox's test showed an "average increase of +8.5% in their qualified play through rate. Some games had an increase of +50%." Best practices: keep essential elements away from the bottom of the image (overlaid by metadata), use "unique" imagery "on what players should expect," use theme and colour to signal genre. — [Official: Thumbnails doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/thumbnails.md)
- The personalization system is a multi-armed bandit, not a classic A/B test: it "figures out which thumbnail among about five choices works best for each audience segment within hours and delivers the best thumbnail to each user." — [Press: GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/); rollout announcement [Official: DevForum "Get your thumbnails ready for thumbnail personalization"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/get-your-thumbnails-ready-for-thumbnail-personalization/3226599)
- **Icon A/B testing is not available**; developers test icon candidates by uploading them as thumbnails into the personalization test. — [Community: DevForum "Are you able to A/B test game icons"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/are-you-able-to-ab-test-game-icons/3339468)
- Metadata rules that affect recommendation eligibility (accuracy, uniqueness, no giveaway language, no mismatched thumbnails) — see Key Question 1, [Official: Discovery doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)

**Acquisition analytics (official)**
- Sources tracked: "Recommended Experiences section on Home," "Continue Play on Home," "Today's Picks on Home," "Favorites and all other traffic on Home," "Friend Activity section on Home," organic search, recommendations on Experience Details pages, Charts and all sorts, "Sponsored experiences and takeovers," "Search ads and portal ads," teleports from other games, share links, social/influencer referrals, and unknown/external. — [Official: Acquisition doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/acquisition.md) (published at https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)
- Funnel metrics: "Users with plays — Unique new users with plays on the game"; "Conversion rate — Percent of new users who played your game after viewing an impression"; "D7 retention — Percentage of new players acquired in the date range who played again on the 8th day (D8) after their first session ended"; "7D playtime per user (cumulative)"; "30D payer conversion"; "30D revenue per user." Guidance: "Get your session time ... and day 1 retention ... to be comparable or above your similar game benchmarks," improve metadata, engage community, partner with creators, and compare share-link performance to find the highest-converting channels. — [Official: Acquisition doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/acquisition.md)
- Retention definitions: D1/D7/D30 = users who first played on a date and returned the next day / after 1 week / after 1 month; improvement guidance: Day 1 → "core loop," "first-time user experience," "performance"; Day 7 → "progression system"; Day 30 → "ending system," regular content updates and "social mechanics." — [Official: Retention doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)
- Monetization definitions: ARPDAU = "Average revenue per daily active user"; ARPPU = "Average revenue per paying user"; payer conversion rate = "Percent of daily active users who are also paying users." — [Official: Monetization doc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/monetization.md)
- Share links for off-platform attribution: creators can create links, "share it on social and through other off-platform channels to track which channels bring in the most engaged players," and "use custom LaunchData to create in-experience offers for those who join from a share link"; the Acquisition dashboard can be viewed "by acquisition source or by share links." — [Official: DevForum "Analytics: Track your off platform user acquisition with share links"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-track-your-off-platform-user-acquisition-with-share-links/3504691) (Feb 2025)

### Inferences
- The combination of (a) play-through rate being a top-tier ranking signal, (b) a free bandit test that measures qualified play-through per thumbnail, and (c) no icon test means the cheapest, highest-leverage pre-launch work is producing 3–5 genuinely different gameplay thumbnails (and a compliant gameplay video) and letting personalization pick winners — this costs $0 of the budget.
- A cinematic-cutscene game must be careful with the video rule set: free-camera cinematic angles are allowed, but graphics enhancement beyond what players see, narration, and lyrical music are prohibited, and mismatched metadata is a documented de-prioritisation trigger.
- The Acquisition dashboard already separates "Sponsored experiences" from "Recommended Experiences," so the user can directly compare D7 retention and 30D payer conversion of paid vs organic cohorts; this is the right instrument for deciding whether to keep spending.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox benchmarks for experience-page conversion rate (impression → play) or like-ratio effects were found; the Acquisition page shows a "similar games" benchmark in-product but the comparison methodology is not documented in the fetched docs.
- The 2026 GameAnalytics report figures could not be read (domain blocked); the 2025 figures are the latest verified.
- No official statement on whether likes/favorites are ranking inputs; they are absent from the published signal list.

## Key Question 3: Roblox Ads Manager in 2026 — formats, bidding, costs, minimums, targeting, creative, traffic quality, known issues, immersive ads/portals, funding, external attribution

### Takeaway
A creator's realistic paid option is Sponsored Experiences (Home + search tiles, cost-per-play, automated bidding, 13+ audience only, funded by card at $1 per ad credit for 18+ or by Robux at 263 Robux per credit for 13+), plus Search Ads (second-price keyword auction). Community-reported cost per play ranges from ~$0.04–0.07 at auction floor to a reported 2026 average of ~$0.22–0.45; a $400 budget therefore buys roughly 900–1,800 plays (or a few thousand at the low end), delivered only to 13+ users, with plays/earnings attributed up to 30 days later and fraud refunds after 14–16 days. Rewarded video/immersive ads are a *publisher* revenue feature requiring 2,000 unique monthly visitors, not an acquisition channel; external links are attributed via share links.

### Cited Findings

**Formats and placements (official)**
- Roblox-served formats: "Immersive ads" (games/videos/images inside publisher ad units), "Search ads" ("Sponsored game tiles based off of keywords and relevance"), "Sponsored games" ("All ages, 9+, 13+ games" shown "Within the Sponsored category on the Home page"), "Sponsored items" (marketplace). Violating advertising standards "may suspend your game and/or account." — [Official: Advertise on Roblox doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox.md)

### Inferences
- **Budget arithmetic [Estimate]**: $400 ≈ 400 ad credits (card) or ≈ 105,200 Robux at 263 Robux/credit if funded from earned/purchased Robux. At the community-reported 2026 average CPP of $0.22–0.45, $400 buys ≈ 890–1,800 plays; at the auction-floor range of $0.04–0.07/play it would buy ≈ 5,700–10,000 plays, but that range is 2023–24 and for games that win cheaply (high CTR, unique). A sensible planning band is ~1,000–3,000 plays.
- Because the card minimum is effectively $5 and the first $100 is billed on accrual, the $400 can be split into several $50–100 test flights (different thumbnails, genres, device targets) rather than one burst — the docs' refund and 48-hour-latency rules mean each flight needs ~3 days before judging.
- Since Ads Manager cannot reach under-13s and new experiences are initially 16+-only anyway, an anime-combat game's paid audience is 13+/16+, which is where its organic audience also sits; targeting the "Anime"/"Fighting & Battlegrounds"/"RPG" genres and PC + mobile devices is the only targeting that matters at this budget.
- Funding ads with Robux is strictly worse than card at the user's scale: 263 Robux per credit means Robux bought at ~80/$ cost ~$3.29 per credit; only Earned Robux (which would otherwise DevEx at $0.0038 × 263 ≈ $1.00) are roughly at parity.

- No data on retention of sponsored vs organic cohorts beyond single-developer anecdotes.

## Key Question 4: Recoup math — DevEx rate, marketplace fee, Robux prices, typical ARPDAU/ARPPU/payer conversion, and how many players are needed to earn back ~$530

### Takeaway
To cash out $530 the developer needs ≈139,500 Earned Robux at the $0.0038 standard DevEx rate; because Roblox keeps 30% of pass/dev-product sales, players must spend ≈199,000 Robux gross — about $2,490 of Robux at the web price of 80 Robux/$ — and the first cash-out requires a 30,000-Robux minimum. Community ARPDAU estimates for small/mid games (~$0.003–0.025 net per DAU-day) imply tens of thousands of DAU-days; the ~1,000–3,000 paid plays the budget buys cannot recoup it directly under any plausible payer-conversion/ARPPU assumption.

### Cited Findings

**DevEx (official)**
- Standard rate "0.0038 per 1 Earned Robux" ($114 for 30,000) for Robux earned on/after 5 Sept 2025 10am PT; legacy "0.0035" for earlier balances, which "must cash out at the old exchange rate before you can cash out any Earned Robux at the current exchange rate"; U.S. 18+ rate "0.0054" for "eligible purchases from age-verified U.S. players"; "minimum of 30,000 Earned Robux" to cash out; eligibility: 13+, verified email, DevEx portal account, W-9 or W-8 on file, compliance with Terms/Community Standards; Earned Robux include developer products, passes, subscriptions, private servers, paid access, marketplace items, creator rewards and Creator Store sales; payouts ~10 business days first time, ~5 thereafter. — [Official: Developer Exchange doc (GitHub source)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/developer-exchange.md) (published at https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange)
- The U.S. 18+ rate is effective 8 June 2026, pays "42% more on eligible in-game spend generated by age-checked players 18 and older in the United States," is blended with the standard rate per cash-out, and (per the announcement) applies to experiences using the R15 avatar framework. — [Official: DevForum "Introducing the US 18+ DevEx Rate"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091); explainer [Community: devex.gg](https://devex.gg/18-plus-devex-rate)
- Premium membership is not among the current DevEx requirements. — [Community guide: ExitLag](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/devex-roblox/); official list above.

**Fees and Robux prices**
- Game passes and developer products carry a unified 30% marketplace fee; the creator receives 70% "regardless of whether or not you have Premium." — [Official: DevForum "Unified Marketplace Fee for Dev Products and Game Passes"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unified-marketplace-fee-for-dev-products-and-game-passes/507109) (2020, still current per calculators such as [mathgyro](https://mathgyro.com/roblox-tax-calculator/))
- Player purchase prices (web/PC): ~800 Robux for $9.99 (≈80 Robux/$, ≈$0.0125 per Robux); 10,000 Robux for $99.99 (≈100/$); Premium members get ~10% more; mobile app prices differ. — [Community price guides: generalistprogrammer](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tools/usd-to-robux/10); [RoWatcher 2026 bundle guide](https://rowatcher.com/news/robux-prices-bundles-2026-guide)

**Monetization benchmarks (community / third-party estimates — no official benchmarks published)**
- "A conservatively monetized game — think a mid-tier simulator or an obby with a VIP gamepass — might see ... ARPDAU somewhere around $0.003 to $0.006 after the full conversion chain, while a well-monetized game ... might hit $0.015–$0.025 ARPDAU"; "at 1,000 concurrent players, assuming roughly 4x that in daily actives ... 4,000 DAU generating perhaps $12–$24 per day in developer-net Robux equivalent. That's $360–$720 per month"; "If your game has fewer than 200–300 concurrent players, you're not in the zone where optimization matters much yet." — [Third-party estimate: RoWatcher "DevEx Math in 2026"](https://rowatcher.com/news/devex-math-in-2026-what-you-actually-take-home-per-1-000-players)
- Payer conversion: "average 1–3%, with top titles around 8%"; by genre: "Simulator/Tycoon 2–4%, RPG/Adventure 1.5–2.5%, Roleplay 1–2%, Obby 0.5–1.5%, Social/Hangout 0.5–1%"; a separate calculator claims "Roblox average ARPDAU ranges $0.05–$0.15" (likely gross player spend, inconsistent with the net figures above — flag). — [Third-party estimates: calculatorkaro revenue estimator](https://calculatorkaro.com/roblox-game-revenue-estimator/); [generalistprogrammer monetization guide](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/roblox-game-monetization-complete-revenue-strategy-guide)
- Roblox's platform-level commentary notes "a decline in per hour monetization most notably with younger cohorts in the U.S. and Canada" in Q2 2026 as engagement shifted to lower-monetizing games. — [Official: Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- Developers report payer conversion and ARPDAU *falling* as a game grows (more casual traffic dilutes the base). — [Community: DevForum "Payer Conversion Rate and ARPDAU decreasing as game gains more popularity"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/payer-conversion-rate-and-arpdau-decreasing-as-game-gains-more-popularity/3898921)

### Inferences (all [Estimate], arithmetic from the cited rates)
- Net Robux needed: $530 ÷ $0.0038 = **139,474 Earned Robux**. Gross player spend needed at a 30% fee: 139,474 ÷ 0.70 = **199,249 Robux**. In player money at 80 Robux/$ (web): **≈ $2,490** (≈ $1,990 if every buyer used the 10,000-Robux bundle at 100/$). The first DevEx request cannot be made until 30,000 Earned Robux (≈ $114) have accumulated; $530 is ~4.6 such minimum chunks.
- If part of the spend comes from age-checked 18+ US players at $0.0054, the Robux needed falls proportionally (all-18+-US case: 98,148 net / 140,212 gross Robux ≈ $1,750 of player spend) — unlikely for an anime game whose audience is mostly teens and non-US.
- Via ARPDAU: at RoWatcher's net $0.003–0.006 per DAU-day, recouping $530 needs ≈ 88,000–177,000 DAU-days (e.g., 300 DAU sustained for 10–20 months); at the "well-monetized" $0.015–0.025, ≈ 21,000–35,000 DAU-days (300 DAU for 2.5–4 months, or 1,000 DAU for 3–5 weeks).
- Via the paid cohort alone: $400 → ~900–1,800 plays (at $0.22–0.45 CPP) → at 1.5–2.5% payer conversion (RPG band) ≈ 14–45 payers → each would have to spend 4,400–14,000 Robux ($55–$180) for the ad spend alone to be recouped from them. That is not a realistic ARPPU for a new indie game, so recoup depends entirely on organic growth that the ads do not directly cause.
- Practical implication: the budget should be judged as an *information* purchase (does the experience retain and convert at benchmark levels?) rather than as an investment expected to return cash; the data from the Acquisition dashboard after ~$100–150 of spend is sufficient to answer that.

### Gaps
- Roblox publishes no genre-level ARPDAU/ARPPU/payer-conversion benchmarks; all figures above are third-party models of unknown provenance. In-product "similar games" benchmarks (percentile bands) exist in Creator Hub analytics but their values are not public.
- No data specific to anime/isekai or "battlegrounds" games' monetization was found.
- Current mobile Robux price tiers (after 2025 app-store price changes) were not verified.

## Key Question 5: Seasonality and timing — traffic peaks, launch days/hours, and launching against big updates

### Takeaway
Roblox's own filings say engagement peaks in June–August, dips September–November, and bookings peak in Q4 around the holidays; the Discovery doc says weekly traffic "usually peaks on a Saturday," and the all-time concurrency record (47.3M) fell on a Saturday afternoon in August 2025 driven by two mega-games' events. The current date (1 Oct 2026) sits in the documented post-summer trough. Day-of-week/hour advice (Friday afternoon–weekend, US evening) is community lore, and Roblox explicitly lists "competition from other successful games" as a factor that suppresses a game's impressions.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox 10-K: the company "tends to generate higher levels of bookings in the fourth quarter of the year primarily due to the end-of-year holiday season"; it "typically sees higher levels of engagement in the months of June, July, and August ... and lower levels of engagement in the post-summer months of September, October, and November"; other seasonal periods include "Lunar New Year, Easter, and Ramadan, as well as school holidays around the world." — [Official: Roblox Form 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)

### Inferences
- October–November is the weakest organic window of the year by Roblox's own disclosure; a launch now competes for fewer impressions, but a soft launch in the trough with a content update timed for the December holiday peak (when both DAU and spending rise) matches the 28-day retention window the algorithm rewards.
- The documented Saturday peak plus the recommendation that signals are measured on organic Home users suggests timing the *organic* push (update, thumbnails, event tag for Today's Picks curators) for late Friday/Saturday US time, while the *paid* flights can run on weekdays when auction competition is lower — the two need not coincide.
- Big-game "event wars" absorb attention and raise auction prices; checking the live charts for a scheduled mega-update on the chosen launch weekend is a cheap precaution, though no source quantifies the effect on a small game.

### Gaps
- No official hour-by-hour traffic data; the only breakdown is a 2022 community thread.
- No evidence quantifying how a major game's update affects a small game's impressions or ad costs.
- Israel-specific or Hebrew-speaking audience traffic patterns were not found.
